    Statements this evidence does not support

    Kept on the page on purpose: the console is where a conditional number turns into a claim.

    • “The system reduces churn by X%” or “increases ROI” for Orange — the interval crosses zero and there is no real margin or cost ledger.
    • “Uplift modelling beats risk modelling” — the evidence here says the opposite on Orange and is conditional on the synthetic mechanism elsewhere.
    • “Randomisation was recovered by estimating e(X)” — reweighting collapses the effective sample size to 3.9.
    • “Orange is not a randomised experiment” — the audit rejects pooled-IID exchangeability, not the per-campaign design the source paper describes.
    • “The pipeline is safe” because the mutation suite passes — it proves 24 declared faults are caught.
    • “Coverage is 95%” from the smoke run — one scenario is not the coverage gate.
    • “Production ready” — no partner, no prospective trial, no serving path for a policy bundle.
